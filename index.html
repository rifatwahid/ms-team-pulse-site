The problem

Teams doesn't know the difference between idle and thinking.

A few minutes without a click or keystroke and your status quietly drops to Away — even mid-call, mid-read, mid-thought. MS Team Pulse doesn't touch your camera, your microphone, or a single message. It just keeps the tab's own activity clock ticking, on a schedule you control.

How it works

Status

One switch. Nothing to remember.

Turn it on when you sit down, off when you're done. That's the entire commitment — no accounts, no setup wizard.

Timing

Choose the rhythm.

Pick how often the signal goes out. Faster for back-to-back calls, slower if you just want the safety net.
