The problem

Teams and Slack don't know the difference between idle and thinking.

A few minutes without a click or keystroke and your status quietly drops to Away — even mid-call, mid-read, mid-thought. StatusPulse doesn't touch your camera, your microphone, or a single message. It just keeps the tab's own activity clock ticking, on a schedule you control.

Support

Stuck on something?

“Test now” says no Teams or Slack tab was found, but I have one open.

Refresh that tab once after installing or reloading the extension. Chrome only injects the content script into tabs opened after that point.

Which sites does this work on?

teams.microsoft.com and teams.live.com (work/school and personal accounts), plus app.slack.com and *.slack.com.

Does it read my messages or calls?

No. It only dispatches a synthetic mouse/keyboard event on the page itself — nothing is read, logged, or sent anywhere.

How do I turn it off?

Open the popup and flip the toggle at the top. That's the entire control — no accounts, no settings to hunt for.
